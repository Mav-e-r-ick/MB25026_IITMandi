The challenge's "sandbox rules" require anonymised, open-source or synthetic data. CGMacros and ShanghaiT2DM are
de-identified, openly published research datasets. No private patient data is used anywhere.

## Real vs synthetic in the app

| Shown in the dashboard | Real or synthetic |
|---|---|
| Patients `S-01` … `S-06` | **Synthetic.** Generated by `src/gtwin/simulate.py`. Profiles, diagnoses, medications and meals are invented. |
| Patients `CGMacros-0xx` | **Real, de-identified** CGM readings plus age, sex, HbA1c and diabetes status from CGMacros. Dates shifted by the dataset creators. |
| Forecasts, risks, intervals, alerts | Model output (simulated), never measured data. |
| What-if scenarios | Model scenarios with assumed (not fitted) meal parameters. |
